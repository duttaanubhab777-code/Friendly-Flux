3D Geometry Solver

Three-Dimensional Geometry Solver

Points, lines, planes, angles, distances — pick a problem type and plug in your numbers.

    How to type things

    • A point: three numbers separated by commas — e.g. 1,2,3 or -2,0,5
    • Direction ratios / normal vector: same format — e.g. 1,-1,2
    • You can use fractions, √ and π inside a coordinate too — e.g. sqrt(2),1/2,-pi
